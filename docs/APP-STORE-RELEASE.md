## Proposed listing text

### Name

MC Labor Sources

### Subtitle

Assignments and work hours

### Promotional text

Keep your workday organized with job information, clock in and out, timesheets, safety updates, and messages from MC Labor Sources.

### Description

MC Labor Sources helps employees stay connected with their work assignments and manage their workday.

• View assigned jobs, job site details, and contact information.
• Clock in and out with location verification.
• Review work hours and timesheets.
• Read safety bulletins and important company messages.
• Receive notifications about work updates.

An employee or supervisor account provided by MC Labor Sources is required. Available features depend on your role and company settings.

Location access is used to verify clock-in and clock-out locations. Notification and photo library permissions support work alerts and saving downloaded timesheet images.

### Keywords

employee,work,assignments,timesheet,clock in,clock out,labor,safety

### Category suggestion

Business
